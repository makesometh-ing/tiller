# V1-08: Open at login

Work file: [F21](../work/F21-open-at-login.md)

## What to build

- The General page in the settings window, as in the approved mockup in [settings-v2.html](../mockups/settings-v2.html).
- An "Open at login" switch on the General page. It is off by default. It uses `SMAppService.mainApp`.
- The switch shows the state from macOS. A change in System Settings is shown in the switch.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] With the switch on, Tiller is listed in System Settings → General → Login Items.
- [ ] Tiller opens after a log out and a log in.

## Proof

Screenshots of the General page and of Login Items, and the log after a log in.
